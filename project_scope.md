# SWIX Website — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Document Purpose

This document defines the scope, audience, content requirements, functionality, and acceptance criteria for the SWIX marketing website. It is the source of truth for writing website copy and designing the initial four-page site.

The website will explain SWIX's staffing, recruitment, and career-support services and encourage prospective candidates, students, experienced job seekers, and employers to contact the company.

## 2. Project Summary

SWIX is a staffing, recruitment, and career-support company with experience supporting people and employers connected to opportunities in the United States, United Kingdom, and Canada. These markets describe experience, not an exclusive service boundary.

The public website will present SWIX as a professional, supportive, and trustworthy partner for:

- Student and recent-graduate career support.
- Experienced job-seeker career support.
- Staffing and recruitment assistance for employers.
- Job-search planning and career guidance.
- Human-led résumé review and rewriting, supported by appropriate AI-assisted quality checks.
- Candidate-authorized job searching and application support across relevant job platforms.
- Interview coordination and scheduling support.
- Mock interviews and interview preparation.
- Ongoing candidate support throughout the job search and placement journey.

The first release will contain four pages:

1. Home
2. About
3. Services
4. Contact

## 3. Product Goals

The website must:

- Clearly explain what SWIX does and who it serves.
- Build trust with candidates and prospective employer clients.
- Present services in plain, professional language.
- Make it easy for visitors to choose the help they need.
- Generate qualified enquiries through a contact form, email, or phone.
- Show the practical problems SWIX solves, including résumé quality, time-consuming job searches, and interview uncertainty.
- Build confidence through verified placement success stories shared with candidate consent.
- Establish a foundation for future job listings and employer resources without requiring them in the first release.

## 4. Non-Goals

The first release will not include:

- A public job board or searchable vacancy database.
- Candidate or employer accounts.
- Résumé uploads or document management.
- Online payments or service checkout.
- Self-service appointment scheduling or calendar integration on the website. SWIX may still coordinate candidate interviews as part of its service.
- A blog, news section, or resource library.
- Live chat or an AI chatbot.
- Automated candidate matching.
- Applicant tracking system integration.
- Detailed immigration or visa services.
- Any public mention of background-verification services or private interview-support services.

These items require a future scope decision before being added.

## 5. Public Service Boundaries

The website may publicly discuss only the approved services defined in this document.

### Approved public services

- Staffing and recruitment for employers.
- Career guidance for students, recent graduates, and experienced professionals.
- Job-search strategy and preparation.
- Human-led résumé review, improvement, and rewriting with AI used only as an assisting quality-control tool.
- Candidate-authorized job discovery and application support for suitable roles.
- Interview coordination and scheduling support between candidates and employers or recruiters.
- Mock interviews and interview coaching.
- Ongoing support during a candidate's job search and placement process.

### Excluded from public website content

- Background verification.
- Undisclosed or private interview support.
- Visa extension assistance or immigration-process support.

The excluded services must not appear in page copy, metadata, structured data, FAQs, testimonials, images, image descriptions, contact-form options, or calls to action.

Internal knowledge of an excluded service does not authorize its inclusion on the website.

## 6. Positioning

### Core positioning statement

SWIX connects talent and opportunity through practical recruitment expertise and personalized career support for students, graduates, experienced professionals, and employers, with market experience connected to the USA, UK, and Canada.

### Primary value proposition

Candidates receive practical, personalized support to improve their résumé, reduce repetitive job-search work, identify relevant opportunities, manage applications, and prepare confidently for real hiring conversations. Employers receive help identifying and connecting with suitable talent.

### Brand promise

SWIX provides clear guidance, honest feedback, and consistent support throughout the recruitment or job-search journey.

### Claims policy

The website must not claim or imply:

- Guaranteed employment, interviews, offers, salaries, or placement timelines.
- Guaranteed employer access or exclusive vacancies unless verifiably true.
- Guaranteed visa, immigration, or legal outcomes.
- That SWIX is a law firm, immigration adviser, educational institution, or government agency.
- That résumé rewriting or interview coaching can bypass an employer's hiring requirements.
- That SWIX will apply to every available role or that every submitted application will lead to an interview.
- That AI alone writes or approves candidate résumés when the service is presented as human-led.
- Partnerships, accreditations, success rates, office locations, or years of experience that have not been verified.

Use language such as “support,” “prepare,” “guide,” and “help improve” instead of “guarantee,” “ensure,” or “secure.”

## 7. Target Audiences

### 7.1 Students and recent graduates

People entering the job market who need help understanding roles, improving their résumé, preparing for interviews, and building a practical job-search plan.

Common needs:

- Understanding where to begin.
- Translating education and projects into employable strengths.
- Creating a professional résumé.
- Reducing time spent searching and applying across separate platforms.
- Practising interview responses.
- Building confidence and consistency during the search.

### 7.2 Experienced job seekers

Professionals seeking a new role, returning to work, changing industries, or repositioning their experience for a different market.

Common needs:

- Clarifying career direction.
- Improving résumé relevance and presentation.
- Communicating achievements effectively.
- Finding and tracking relevant opportunities efficiently.
- Preparing for role-specific interviews.
- Maintaining a structured job-search approach.

### 7.3 Employers and hiring teams

Organizations looking for recruitment support and access to relevant candidates.

Common needs:

- Understanding SWIX's recruitment approach.
- Discussing role and candidate requirements.
- Finding qualified candidates efficiently.
- Having a responsive recruitment contact.

### Geographic audience

The website may reference experience connected to the USA, UK, and Canada. Copy must present these as markets SWIX has worked with—not as the only places where someone may enquire or as guaranteed service availability.

## 8. Primary User Journeys

### Candidate journey

1. A visitor arrives from search, social media, or a referral.
2. The visitor quickly recognizes that SWIX supports students, graduates, and experienced professionals.
3. The visitor sees their résumé, job-search, or interview problem reflected in clear language.
4. The visitor reviews relevant services and understands what SWIX can manage and what still requires their participation or approval.
5. The visitor sees an honest, low-pressure call to action.
6. The visitor submits an enquiry or uses a published contact method.

### Employer journey

1. An employer arrives on the Home or Services page.
2. The employer sees that SWIX provides staffing and recruitment support.
3. The employer understands that services begin with a discussion of hiring needs.
4. The employer contacts SWIX with organization and role information.

## 9. Information Architecture

The primary navigation must contain only:

- Home
- About
- Services
- Contact

The global header must include a prominent contact call to action. The footer must repeat essential navigation and verified company contact information.

## 10. Page Requirements

### 10.1 Home page

#### Purpose

Explain the offer quickly, establish credibility, serve both candidate and employer audiences, and direct visitors to the right next step.

#### Required sections

1. **Hero**
   - Clear headline describing staffing, recruitment, and career support.
   - Short supporting statement describing USA, UK, and Canada as market experience, not fixed service regions.
   - Primary CTA: “Explore Our Services.”
   - Secondary CTA: “Contact SWIX.”

2. **Who we help**
   - Students and recent graduates.
   - Experienced professionals.
   - Employers and hiring teams.

3. **Problems candidates face and how SWIX helps**
   - **Problem: generic or low-quality AI-generated résumés.** Candidates may waste time correcting unfocused, repetitive, or inaccurate documents that do not represent their real experience.
   - **SWIX solution:** an experienced professional leads the writing and editing process. AI may assist with spelling, consistency, comparison, and quality checks, but a person reviews the final résumé for clarity, accuracy, and relevance.
   - **Problem: fragmented, repetitive job hunting.** Candidates may spend hours searching separate platforms, reviewing similar listings, and repeating application steps.
   - **SWIX solution:** with the candidate's authorization, SWIX searches agreed platforms, identifies suitable roles, helps tailor and submit applications, tracks progress, and supports interview scheduling.
   - **Problem: interview anxiety and uncertainty.** Candidates may not know what to expect, how to structure answers, or how to communicate their experience confidently.
   - **SWIX solution:** practical interview guidance, role-relevant preparation, mock interviews, and constructive feedback before the real interview.
   - **Problem: losing direction during a long job search.** Candidates may struggle to decide what to improve or do next.
   - **SWIX solution:** an agreed job-search plan, regular guidance, and ongoing support focused on the candidate's career goal.

4. **Core services overview**
   - Staffing and recruitment.
   - Career guidance and job-search support.
   - Résumé review and rewriting.
   - Managed job search and application support.
   - Mock interviews and interview preparation.

5. **How SWIX works**
   - Understand the visitor's needs.
   - Create a practical plan.
   - Prepare the résumé and candidate profile.
   - Search and apply for agreed, suitable opportunities with authorization.
   - Prepare for interviews and coordinate next steps.
   - Provide ongoing support through the agreed service period.

6. **Why choose SWIX**
   - Personalized guidance.
   - Experienced human review supported by responsible technology.
   - Less repetitive job-search work for the candidate.
   - Practical, honest feedback.
   - Support for different career stages.
   - International market awareness.

7. **Placement success stories**
   - Show real candidate journeys from initial challenge to placement outcome.
   - Explain what support SWIX provided without taking credit for the candidate's own skills and effort.
   - Share an offer or placement celebration only with the candidate's explicit written permission.
   - Use only verified facts and redact compensation, employer, location, or personal details when requested.
   - Never fabricate a story, quote, employer name, offer letter, or result. If no approved story is available, do not use a fake placeholder presented as real.

8. **Final CTA**
   - Invite candidates and employers to start a conversation.

#### Home-page content rule

The hero must communicate the business within a few seconds. Avoid vague slogans that do not explain the service.

### 10.2 About page

#### Purpose

Build trust by explaining SWIX's mission, approach, values, audiences, and geographic focus.

#### Required sections

1. **Introduction to SWIX**
   - What the company does.
   - Who it serves.
   - Where it focuses.

2. **Mission**
   - Help people approach career opportunities with clarity, preparation, and confidence while helping employers connect with suitable talent.

3. **Approach**
   - Listen before recommending.
   - Tailor support to the individual or hiring need.
   - Give practical and honest guidance.
   - Maintain clear communication.

4. **Values**
   - Integrity.
   - Respect.
   - Clarity.
   - Responsiveness.
   - Continuous improvement.

5. **Who SWIX supports**
   - Students and recent graduates.
   - Experienced job seekers.
   - Employers.

6. **CTA**
   - Invite the visitor to view services or contact the company.

#### About-page content rule

Do not invent a founding story, team biographies, certifications, office locations, placement figures, or years of experience. Add them only after the business supplies and approves the facts.

### 10.3 Services page

#### Purpose

Give visitors enough detail to identify the relevant service and submit an informed enquiry.

#### Required service groups

1. **Staffing and recruitment for employers**
   - Initial hiring-needs discussion.
   - Candidate sourcing and screening support.
   - Coordination and communication during the recruitment process.
   - CTA: “Discuss Your Hiring Needs.”

2. **Student and graduate career support**
   - Career-direction discussion.
   - Job-search planning.
   - Résumé support.
   - Interview preparation.
   - CTA: “Start Your Career Conversation.”

3. **Experienced professional support**
   - Career-change or job-search strategy.
   - Positioning experience for relevant roles.
   - Résumé improvement.
   - Interview preparation.
   - CTA: “Talk About Your Next Move.”

4. **Résumé review and rewriting**
   - Review structure, clarity, relevance, and presentation.
   - Improve how skills, experience, projects, and achievements are communicated.
   - Tailor recommendations to the candidate's goals.
   - Position the service as professional, human-led writing and editing.
   - Explain that AI may assist with proofreading, consistency, comparison, or quality checks, while an experienced team member remains responsible for the final review.
   - Require the candidate to confirm that all qualifications, experience, achievements, and dates are truthful.
   - No promise that a rewritten résumé will produce interviews or offers.

5. **Managed job search and application support**
   - Agree on target roles, locations, working arrangements, compensation expectations, and other important criteria with the candidate.
   - Search relevant job platforms and employer career pages for suitable positions.
   - Help tailor application materials and submit applications only with clear candidate authorization.
   - Maintain an application record so the candidate can see which roles were selected and submitted.
   - Help coordinate recruiter communication and interview scheduling without impersonating the candidate.
   - Do not accept declarations, assessments, legal terms, or attestations on the candidate's behalf when they require the candidate personally.
   - Do not misrepresent identity, work authorization, location, skills, employment history, education, or availability.
   - Follow the terms and application rules of each job platform and employer.
   - No promise regarding the number of suitable roles, applications, interviews, or offers unless a written service package defines a controllable deliverable.

6. **Mock interviews and preparation**
   - Practice common and role-relevant questions.
   - Improve answer structure and communication.
   - Provide constructive feedback and preparation guidance.
   - No participation in real interviews on the candidate's behalf and no misrepresentation of candidate identity, skills, or experience.

7. **Ongoing job-search support**
   - Check-ins, preparation, and practical guidance during the candidate's active search.
   - Describe this as ongoing support, not guaranteed support “until placement” without limits.
   - Any duration, package, eligibility, or pricing terms must be defined and approved before publication.

8. **Placement celebration and success-story sharing**
   - Congratulate the candidate and, when useful, document the journey and support provided.
   - Publish a candidate quote, placement result, image, employer name, or offer detail only with specific written permission.
   - Allow the candidate to approve the final story and request reasonable redaction or removal.
   - Present the story as an individual outcome, not proof that every client will receive the same result.

#### Service-page content rule

Each service must explain:

- Who it is for.
- What problem it addresses.
- What the visitor can expect.
- What it does not guarantee.
- What action to take next.

### 10.4 Contact page

#### Purpose

Provide a simple, trustworthy path for candidate and employer enquiries.

#### Required sections

1. **Contact introduction**
   - Invite visitors to explain their career goal or hiring need.
   - State the normal response time only after the business confirms it.

2. **Enquiry form**
   - Full name — required.
   - Email address — required.
   - Phone number — optional.
   - Candidate location — required free-text field; do not restrict enquiries to USA, UK, or Canada.
   - Enquiry type — required:
     - Student or recent graduate support.
     - Experienced professional support.
     - Résumé support.
     - Managed job search and application support.
     - Mock interview preparation.
     - Employer staffing or recruitment.
     - General enquiry.
   - Message — required.
   - Consent acknowledgement with a link to the applicable privacy notice.

3. **Direct contact options**
   - Verified business email.
   - Verified phone number, if calls are supported.
   - Verified business hours and time zone, if published.

4. **Submission states**
   - Clear success confirmation.
   - Clear, accessible error message with a retry option.
   - Protection against duplicate submissions and spam.

#### Contact-page content rule

The form must not request a passport number, visa documents, government identifiers, financial details, or other unnecessary sensitive information. Résumé upload is outside the first-release scope.

## 11. Content Strategy

### Voice and tone

Content should be:

- Professional but approachable.
- Encouraging without exaggeration.
- Clear, direct, and easy to scan.
- Respectful of students and experienced professionals.
- Confident without using pressure or fear.
- Written in internationally understandable English.

### Preferred language

Use:

- “Career support”
- “Job-search guidance”
- “Interview preparation”
- “Résumé review and rewriting”
- “Human-led résumé writing with AI-assisted quality checks”
- “Managed job search and application support”
- “Candidate-authorized applications”
- “Interview coordination”
- “Staffing and recruitment support”
- “Students and recent graduates”
- “Experienced professionals”
- “Ongoing support throughout your job search”

Avoid:

- “Guaranteed placement”
- “100% job assurance”
- “Guaranteed interview”
- “Guaranteed visa”
- “Pay after placement” unless a reviewed commercial offer actually uses that model
- “Best,” “leading,” or “number one” without independently verifiable evidence
- Language suggesting SWIX can attend, control, or complete a real interview for a candidate
- Language suggesting applications are submitted without the candidate's knowledge or authorization

### Problem–solution messaging framework

Candidate-facing content should follow this sequence:

1. Acknowledge the real frustration without insulting or blaming the candidate.
2. Explain the cost of the problem in time, confidence, clarity, or missed relevance.
3. Describe the specific work SWIX performs.
4. Explain where human expertise and supporting technology are used.
5. Give the candidate a realistic next step without promising an outcome.

Approved message themes include:

- Spend less time fixing generic résumé output; receive a résumé shaped and reviewed by experienced people, with AI used only for supporting quality checks.
- Reduce repetitive searching across individual job platforms; let SWIX help identify suitable openings and manage authorized applications against agreed criteria.
- Replace interview uncertainty with guided preparation, practice, and constructive feedback.
- Work toward a dream role or career goal with a clearer plan and ongoing support—without implying that a particular job is guaranteed.
- Celebrate successful placements together and share the journey only when the candidate chooses to provide informed written consent.

### Spelling and terminology

Use “résumé” in marketing copy, with “resume” included naturally where useful for search discoverability. Choose one primary English style for general prose and apply it consistently. Preserve official country and organization names as written.

## 12. Calls to Action

Use a small, consistent CTA set:

- Explore Our Services
- Get Career Support
- Discuss Your Hiring Needs
- Contact SWIX
- Start a Conversation

Avoid high-pressure wording and outcome guarantees.

## 13. Functional Requirements

- Responsive header and navigation.
- Mobile navigation with accessible open/close behavior.
- Active navigation state generated at build time.
- Reusable layouts and page sections rendered by Eleventy.
- Reusable problem–solution and success-story components populated only with approved content.
- Contact form with accessible client-side feedback and server-side or provider-side validation.
- Success and error states that do not depend on color alone.
- Verified contact links using `mailto:` and `tel:` where appropriate.
- Static production output in `dist/`.
- No authentication, database, or custom application backend in the first release unless required by the selected form provider.

## 14. Accessibility Requirements

- Meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA as the target standard.
- Support keyboard-only navigation.
- Provide visible focus states and a skip link.
- Use semantic landmarks and logical heading levels.
- Give form fields persistent labels and accessible errors.
- Provide suitable alternative text for meaningful images.
- Maintain sufficient text and interface contrast.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Keep content usable at 200% zoom and on narrow mobile screens.

## 15. SEO Requirements

- Unique page title and meta description for each of the four pages.
- Canonical URLs based on the confirmed production domain.
- Open Graph metadata and a verified social-sharing image.
- Sitemap and intentional `robots.txt`.
- Organization structured data containing only verified public facts.
- Descriptive headings, links, and image alternative text.
- Natural references to staffing, recruitment, career support, résumé services, mock interviews, and relevant market experience; no keyword stuffing.
- No indexing of development or preview deployments.

## 16. Privacy, Compliance, and Trust

- Publish only verified company information and service claims.
- Collect the minimum personal information required to respond to an enquiry.
- Explain how enquiry information is used and link to a privacy notice.
- Obtain appropriate consent before sending marketing communications.
- Do not expose form submissions in URLs, analytics events, or browser logs.
- Do not send résumé or candidate data to an AI service unless the tool, data handling, retention, access, and required consent have been reviewed and approved.
- Maintain a clear record of candidate authorization for managed applications and the roles submitted on the candidate's behalf.
- Review employment-service, privacy, consumer-protection, accessibility, and marketing obligations applicable to each market before launch.
- Make clear that SWIX provides career and recruitment support, not legal or immigration advice.
- Do not publish client logos, candidate stories, testimonials, statistics, or photographs without permission and factual approval.

## 17. Design Direction

The design should feel:

- Trustworthy and established.
- Modern and uncluttered.
- Human and supportive.
- Appropriate for both candidates and business clients.
- Easy to scan on mobile devices.

Use real, approved company and team photography when available. Avoid imagery that implies guaranteed success, government affiliation, or services outside the approved scope. Decorative stock photography should represent the intended audience respectfully and should not be presented as actual clients or employees.

## 18. Success Measures

Initial success will be assessed through:

- Qualified candidate enquiries.
- Qualified employer enquiries.
- Contact-form completion rate.
- Clicks on verified phone and email links.
- Visits from the Home page to the Services and Contact pages.
- Low form-error and abandonment rates.
- Qualitative feedback that visitors understand what SWIX offers.
- Enquiries for résumé writing, managed job-search support, and mock interviews.
- Candidate-approved placement stories collected over time.

No numeric targets should be published or treated as commitments until baseline traffic and enquiry data are available.

## 19. First-Release Acceptance Criteria

The website is ready for launch when:

1. Home, About, Services, and Contact pages are complete and responsive.
2. Each audience can identify relevant support and a clear next action.
3. The Home page clearly presents the résumé, manual job-search, interview-confidence, and career-direction problems together with SWIX's corresponding solutions.
4. All public claims, contact details, geographic references, and service descriptions are approved by the business.
5. Excluded services do not appear anywhere in public output or metadata.
6. At least one verified success story is approved for publication, or the success-story area is withheld until one is available; no fabricated story is used.
7. The contact form submits successfully and provides accessible success and failure feedback.
8. Navigation and interactive controls work with keyboard and touch input.
9. Every page has approved metadata and renders correctly in the production build.
10. The manual verification checklist in `coding_guidelines.md` has been completed.
11. Privacy notice, consent wording, application authorization, and form-data handling have been reviewed before accepting real enquiries.
12. No page promises a job, interview, placement date, salary, visa result, or other outcome SWIX cannot guarantee.

## 20. Required Business Inputs

The following information is required before final copy or launch:

- Approved company name and preferred capitalization.
- Logo and brand assets.
- Brand colors and typography.
- Verified business email and phone number.
- Production domain.
- Confirmed countries, industries, and role types currently supported.
- Whether SWIX serves employers directly in all three target countries.
- Approved explanation of the recruitment process.
- Definition of the managed job-search service: supported platforms, target-role criteria, application volume or cadence, approval workflow, communication process, and record keeping.
- Candidate authorization terms for job searches, application submissions, and interview scheduling.
- Approved AI tools and data-handling rules for résumé quality checks, or confirmation that candidate data will not be processed with AI.
- Exact scope, duration, and commercial terms of ongoing candidate support.
- Form recipient and selected form-processing service.
- Privacy contact and approved privacy notice.
- Business address, hours, and time zone, if they will be published.
- At least one approved placement success story, including written consent and an agreed level of identification, or approval to launch without displaying a story initially.
- Approved testimonials, statistics, accreditations, or partner logos, if any.
- Preferred primary English style and any market-specific wording requirements.

Until these inputs are approved, use clearly marked placeholders and do not invent missing facts.

## 21. Future Considerations

Possible later phases may include:

- Job listings and application workflows.
- Employer-specific landing pages.
- Candidate resources and articles.
- A larger searchable success-story or case-study library with permission.
- Appointment scheduling.
- Résumé upload through a secure provider.
- CRM or applicant-tracking integration.
- Optional market-experience content for the USA, UK, and Canada, without presenting those markets as exclusive service regions.

Each future feature requires separate scope, privacy, security, accessibility, and content review.
